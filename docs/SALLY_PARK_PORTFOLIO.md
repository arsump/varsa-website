# Gulf State Park portfolio coverage test

November 24, 2026. One operator, one storm, 24 named facilities or service areas. The denominator is the November 1, 2020 [official operating bulletin](https://www.alapark.com/sites/default/files/2020-11/Visitor%20Need%20to%20Know_201106.pdf). Count physical facilities and named areas, deduplicate the pier, omit generic park-wide amenities and programs, and roll up the Lodge pool and on-site concessions under their host facilities. The campground and separately listed facilities are distinct service rows, not independent buildings. The bulletin supplies a point-in-time status, not the cause or duration of a closure. No customer input, outreach or deployment.

## Coverage

- **24/24** bulletin snapshot statuses. **9 closed, 4 open with restrictions or alternative access, and 11 open** when Cotton Bayou's reported open amenities are treated as its snapshot status, and Romar's alternate access is treated as restricted. The four restricted rows are Romar, campground, bathhouses, and tennis courts.
- **3/24** verified pre-storm protection or durability work (Lodge, Interpretive Center, pier; pier is a durability upgrade only).
- **4/24** affirmative facility-specific Sally damage (pier, boat launch, Lodge, cabins). Interpretive Center is an additional reported outcome without confirmed damage. Unresolved rows are not undamaged.
- **2/24** documented restoration or reopening milestones plus clearly event-linked project totals (pier and cabins). The cabins total includes a change in capacity. Treat April 15 as the ceremonial opening, not the first day of operation.
- **1/24** pre-storm work + Sally damage + dated recovery milestones + project-level economics (pier). **0/24** verified paid storm-only totals. **0/24** fully documented chains with verified payments.

## What the workflow adds for an operator

Report documented service status across the whole roster. Prioritize known damage and dated service interruptions, distinguish continued operations from recovery, and keep new capacity or upgrades within a project total visible. The strongest recovered histories are suitable for a retrospective capital review. Claim accounting or a causal estimate of protection effectiveness requires additional records. This test measures public evidence coverage, not buyer demand or commercial reuse rights.
