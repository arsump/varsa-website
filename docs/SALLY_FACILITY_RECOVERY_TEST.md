# Public facility recovery record test

Reviewed November 24, 2026. Defined source check across Gulf State Park Pier, Gulf Place beachfront lighting, and Perdido Pass Seawall Park. This is a targeted follow-up, not a representative portfolio estimate.

| Facility | Pre-event evidence | Sally impact and recovery | Economic evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gulf State Park Pier, 20800 East Beach Blvd., Gulf Shores | The [NOAA-hosted restoration report, p. 4–5](https://pub-data.diver.orr.noaa.gov/restoration/Gulf_State_Park_Pier_Renovation_ID239_2022_Annual_Report.pdf) confirms hardwood decking and railing upgrades completed in early September 2020. Durability upgrade; structural hurricane protection and causal effect remain unknown. [Facility address](https://www.alapark.com/parks/gulf-state-park/beaches). | September 16, 2020 storm damaged pier. [State announcement](https://www.alapark.com/sites/default/files/2021-01/Press%20Release_Gulf%20State%20Park%20Pier%20Reopening.V2.pdf) documents partial reopening January 30, 2021, 136 days after landfall. [State reopening notice](https://outdooralabama.com/articles/gulf-state-park-pier-reopens-public) confirms August 19, 2024 reopening, 1,433 days after landfall. The latter interval includes periods of partial operation. | State reports a $13.6M completed repair and enhancement project and [confirms multiple funding sources were used](https://outdooralabama.com/articles/community-leaders-celebrate-renovations-gulf-state-park-pier). FEMA records a $13,629,365.27 federal obligation. Retain separately. Invoice ledger and storm-only paid total unknown. |
| Gulf Place beachfront lighting, Gulf Shores | Existing lighting identified in scope; independently verified pre-event protection unknown. | [City specifications](https://www.gulfshoresal.gov/DocumentCenter/View/9956/2021-0203---Gulf-Place-Lighting-Repairs---ITB?bidId=) identify replacement of damaged beachfront lighting. Completion and reopening unknown in reviewed sources. | [Council packet](https://gulfshoresal.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_02222021-766?packet=true) documents a $299,218 bid and $320,000 proposed ceiling. Insurance reimbursement anticipated; receipts and payments unknown. |
| Perdido Pass Seawall Park, Orange Beach | Existing seawall park; pre-event design or as-built protection unverified. | [Bid scope](https://www.orangebeachal.gov/bids.aspx?bidID=7) covers Sally repairs plus Phase III expansion. A contractor notice references Phase II under FEMA project 181334; the phase mismatch requires verification before an automated join. [Notice](https://beta.creativecirclecdn.com/gulfcoast/files/20240429-144627-240501%20Courier%20E%20edition.pdf). | [Council minutes](https://www.orangebeachal.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/581?fileID=6038) authorize up to $695,597.05 for the combined scope. Storm-only allocation and payment unknown. |

Result: Three named assets with documented damage and a project-level financial figure; one with confirmed pre-event durability work and two dated service milestones; zero verified storm-only paid totals. The [Alabama checkbook](https://open.alabama.gov/spending_checkbook.aspx) advertises unaudited agency/payee payment searches. This review did not establish a checkbook transaction link to a particular repair scope. A verified disbursement would need the contract or project ID, invoice scope and transaction evidence. Record funding source, obligation, approved ceiling, reported project total and verified disbursement as distinct measures. No customer inputs, requests to third parties, site deployment or commercial release.
