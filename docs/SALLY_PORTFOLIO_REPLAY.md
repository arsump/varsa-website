# Hurricane Sally commercial portfolio replay

November 24, 2026. Public-source desk audit against [IBHS's 19 pre-landfall commercial designations](https://ibhs.org/wp-content/uploads/member_docs/fortified-commercial-sally-overview.pdf). The publication gives locations and designation counts, but the reviewed sources do not expose a row-level roster or certificate-to-building crosswalk. Named sites can span several buildings. Report confirmed site leads and leave the exact certificate-level percentage unknown.

Count a site only with a named property, corroborated situs address, dated designation before September 16, 2020, and an affirmative site-specific report of damage or operations. For the cost stage require an actual paid amount attributable to that site and event. Keep cohort summaries, component estimates, nearby facilities and grant budgets outside that count.

**Coverage found:** Three named site-level reports with dated historical designation. Two qualify for the corroborated address-to-outcome chain. Zero address-to-paid-cost chains found. Certificate-level coverage cannot be estimated reliably from these public sources. The denominator describes a selected protected cohort, with no comparison group for causal inference. No independent asset inspection or measured on-site intensity was verified. Review used IBHS reports and announcements, a USACE-hosted-event transcript, park notices, project engineer reporting, city reporting and searches for Sally public assistance and property-specific repair payments.

**Baseline comparison:** A hazard-and-permit screen could flag likely storm exposure or approved work. On the two corroborated addresses, the replay added issuer-backed protection at event time and post-event reporting. It also exposed an operational distinction: a structure reported to have fared well can still be listed as closed. No buyer, lender or insurer was interviewed, and payment demand was not tested.

**Decision:** Public sources support source-linked case histories. Use cohort research for economics where appropriate. Portfolio-grade loss coverage requires a full historical designation roster keyed by building plus an authorized outcome and paid-cost source, with commercial reuse rights. Avoid coding missing payments as zero. No outreach, confidential records, deployment, or release-gate changes.
