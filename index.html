Research roadmap

Performance evidence

Match building use, assembly, age and verified protection timing. Compare similar measured event intensity and track separately documented damage, downtime and recovery. Publish the sample, missing outcomes and uncertainty.

Financial evidence

Link verified installation, repair and interruption costs to the observed outcome and date. Record currency year, geography and coverage. Keep lab studies, field observations and modeled estimates identifiable.
